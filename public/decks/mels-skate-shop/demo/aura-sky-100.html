This is a carbon boot, and Aura builds it to fit very snug before it is moulded. That is not a sizing error, it is the design: the carbon expands in width and volume onto your foot once it has been warmed. The heat-mould is not an upgrade on these — Aura publishes it as a requirement, so plan for it. We size from the Brannock suggested size rather than raw foot length. That suggested size is the high-performance fit; add half a size, 5 mm, if you want a comfort fit. And the rule we hold to at the bench is Aura's own: if the boot feels tight, move up in width before you add length. Width first, length second. Because we import this boot specifically for you, we confirm the size with you on WhatsApp before anything is ordered.

What is a heat-mould?

We warm the boot until the carbon softens, you stand in it in the sock you skate in, and it cools onto your heel, your instep and your ankle. On an Aura it is not a luxury step: Aura publishes the mould as a requirement. On the live site that is a phone call to the shop. Here you book it with the fitting and it happens in the same appointment.

Can I cancel?

Before we order, nothing is committed — we only place the order once you have confirmed the size with us on WhatsApp, and that confirmation is the moment that matters. After we have ordered, the boot is being brought into the country for you specifically, so we are not going to print a policy here that we have not agreed with you. Message us and we will tell you honestly where the order has got to. Cancelling a fitting rather than a boot is simpler, and that policy is on the booking page.
