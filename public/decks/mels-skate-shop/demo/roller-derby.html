FAQ

What size am I?

Measure, do not guess. Heel to wall, standing, both feet, and use the larger one — the Size Finder walks you through it and gives you the size in the brand's own scale. Two honest warnings. Brands differ by 1-2 full sizes for the same foot, so a number off a chart is a starting point, not a fitting. And our size chart carries no table at all for Riedell or Sure-Grip, which are brands a derby skater may well end up in. For those, send us the measurement on WhatsApp and we size you by hand.

Which plate size for my boot?

Plates are sized by wheelbase — axle to axle — against the boot, not by your shoe size. We publish the Pilot F-16 chart on our size chart page: plate sizes 5.0 to 8.5, mapped onto Q-4/Q-6, F-1 and Jackson boot sizes, with plate lengths from 218 mm to 309 mm. Check the size with us before you order. For Roll-Line there is no plate-to-boot table published anywhere we could find, so that one is a WhatsApp conversation: send us the boot and its size and we work it out with you.

What wheel hardness do I want for Roadhouse versus tarmac?

The principle first, because the principle is the part that is always true. A softer compound grips and absorbs, which is what a rough outdoor surface takes out of you; a harder compound slides and rolls faster, which is what a smooth indoor floor rewards. What we will not do is put a number on this page. The right hardness for the floor at Roadhouse, and for the stretch of tarmac outside your house, depends on that surface, on your weight and on how you skate — and it is Melony who confirms it, not a chart. Tell us where you skate and what you weigh, on WhatsApp, and we will pick the set with you.

What does a league kit list need?

The standard derby set is six things: skates, helmet, knee pads, elbow pads, wrist guards and a mouthguard. We carry 27 protective sets, and we would rather build the kit with you than hand you a list — we will tell you straight what we hold and what we bring in. Four South African leagues are running that we know of: Golden City Rollers, Durban Roller Derby, Cape Town Rollergirls and P-Town Roller Derby. What each one requires we are not going to guess at. Per-league kit lists are coming in the build; until then, tell us which league you are joining and we will work through it with you.
