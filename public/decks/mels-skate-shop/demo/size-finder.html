Measure heel to wall

Two minutes, a sheet of paper and a pencil. Do it standing — your foot spreads under your own weight, and that is the foot the boot has to fit.

  1. Put a sheet of paper on a hard floor, one short edge tight against the wall.
  2. Stand on it with your heel against the wall and your weight on that foot.
  3. Mark the paper at the end of your longest toe. That is not always the big toe.
  4. Measure from the wall edge to the mark, in millimetres.
  5. Now do the other foot. Feet differ. Use the larger number.

Measure late in the day, wearing the sock you skate in.
