Before you come

None of this is compulsory. All of it makes the appointment shorter and the fit better.

  1. Measure both feet at home if you can — heel to wall, standing, in millimetres. Bring the numbers.
  2. Wear or bring the socks you skate in. Sock thickness changes the size.
  3. Bring your current skates if you own a pair, even wrong ones. What is wrong with them is the most useful thing you can tell us.
  4. For an ice fitting, bring your blades if you already own them.
  5. For a video consult, have paper, a pen, a tape measure, a wall and a hard floor ready before we call.

Each appointment type lists what to bring; this is the short version of all four.

Every fitting is the same three steps

Measure both feet standing. Try the boot on the plate or blade you would actually skate. Adjust before you leave — lacing, toe stop, heel lift.
